Before you book

Don't see your question here? Ask it on the strategy call.

What makes a Walking Billboard ad different?

Instead of stock photos and generic offers, the ads feature your actual company, crew, and finished projects. Homeowners see a real local business they can trust, which brings in better leads than a coupon ever will.

Do I need to be good on camera?

No. We send you a simple shot list and tips for filming on your phone, so you know exactly what to capture. Most of the ads let your work speak for itself, with you or your crew in a few short clips.

Do you come out and film?

No, and you don't need a film crew. You shoot short clips on your phone using our guide, send them over, and we handle all the editing.

Are the leads shared with other contractors?

No. The leads come from ads running under your company name, so they're yours. We also limit how many companies we take on in each area.

What happens when a lead comes in?

The CRM texts them right away, asks a few qualifying questions, and points them to book an estimate on your calendar. If they go quiet, it keeps following up by text and email so you don't have to.

Do I have to learn new software?

We set everything up for you. You get a mobile app to see new leads, reply to messages, and check your calendar, and we're there to help when you need it.

Do you work outside my state?

Yes. We work with landscaping and hardscaping companies nationwide.
